Manual install instructions

  • iPhone/iPad (Safari):
    1. Tap the Share button (box with arrow)
    2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
    3. Tap "Add" to confirm
  • Android (Chrome):
    1. Tap the menu button (⋮)
    2. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"
    3. Tap "Install" to confirm
  • Other browsers:
    Look for an "Install" option in the menu, or try opening in Chrome/Safari

Note: Make sure you're opening this page directly in your browser, not through a link in another app (Instagram, Facebook, etc.)
